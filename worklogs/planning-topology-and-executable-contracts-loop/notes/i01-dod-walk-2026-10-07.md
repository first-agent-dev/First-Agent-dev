## §1 — DoD-7 failed as written, and the fix

**Found.** `parse_slice_id` is in `plan_ids.__all__` but appeared **nowhere** in the schema
document, let alone §7. `canonical_slice_id` was likewise absent from §7. SLICE3b added
`parse_slice_id` to the public surface (E106 / CT37) and never extended the surface spec.

This is the inverse of the DoD line as literally phrased — the line polices "§7 names
without consumers", and the drift was "exported names missing from §7". The literal line
passes: `plan_commands` and `test_paths` have no direct call site but are reachable through
`commands_for(None)` and `tests_for`, both carrying a named `consumer:` in §7.

**Fixed now** (documentation only, no code change): §7 gains both primitives with their
consumers and, crucially, the reason they differ —

- `parse_slice_id` is **strict**, for use *inside a plan*, where `S2` is ambiguous between a
  slice and a step (E106). Consumer: internal, `_declared_deps`, backing CT10. No external
  consumer; SD-B forbids growing one without naming it.
- `canonical_slice_id` is **lenient**, and only at the eval-report boundary
  (`workflow_controller.py:336`), where a report carries one namespace.

**Not fixed, registered instead (D8).** A guard test asserting `plan_ids.__all__ ⊆ §7` would
stop this recurring. It is not added here: I01 is complete, and bolting an unowned contract
onto a closed increment is the drift this project exists to prevent. It is proposed as a
contract for I02, which is the next consumer of that surface.

## §2 — DoD-2's wording is now stale against E106

Four assertions in `tests/test_plan_ids.py` still mention `S1`/`S2`:
`test_accessors_accept_either_id_grammar` (3) and `test_legacy_short_form_is_canonicalised`
(1). They do **not** assert the old grammar; they pin the *canonicalisation mapping* that
E106 explicitly kept. The DoD phrase "no `S#` assertion survives" predates that decision and
now reads as if it forbade them.

⚠️ **One genuine inconsistency surfaced and is left open.** E106 scoped the surviving
leniency to "the eval-report boundary". But the **accessors** (`commands_for`, `tests_for`,
`section`) are a *second* lenient surface — they canonicalise `S1` too — and E106 never named
them. That is defensible (an accessor call is a single-namespace lookup, like a report), but
it was not decided. Registered as **Q43** rather than settled silently here.

## §3 — DoD-6 passes over text, not over a live run

CT31 parses the plan blocks embedded in `prompt.py` and asserts they yield slices. That
proves the *prompt* is parseable. It does not prove a plan written by a real model, through
the real loop, reaches `workflow_controller.py:332`. That gap is row **D1** of
`deferred-verification-register.md`, owned by I02, and it is the original defect's own
failure mode — so it is the single most important thing I02 must close.
